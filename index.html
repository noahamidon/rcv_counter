Step 1 · Intake

Upload a ballot export.

Drop the raw CSV you downloaded from the Dartmouth survey tool. The counter auto-detects the rank columns and separates distinct ranked-choice races — whether the file has 1 race (Fall) or 3 (Spring).

How races are detected
  • Rank columns match the pattern "Please rank the candidates ... - N".
  • Contiguous blocks of rank columns are grouped into separate races (e.g. the Spring export has three).
  • Each race's true class comes from the "balloted candidates for the Class of XXXX..." column right before it — the rank-column headers themselves can be mislabeled. The tool flags any disagreement.
  • Blank cells are skipped. Empty ballots are counted but don't contribute.
  • Your data never leaves your laptop. There is no backend.

Write-in review

Voters who write in must put their whole ranking — balloted candidates included — in the write-in box. A counted write-in becomes that voter's entire ballot; a dropped one isn't counted.

Candidates on the ballot

Sorted by first-preference tally. Tick to exclude from the count (e.g. a candidate who has already declined).

Seats to fill

EPAC's General Senator races default to 2. If a winner later declines, add a seat from the results screen to promote the next candidate.
